Last updated: November 2026

1. Subscription Terms of Service

1.1 Scope of Services

By subscribing to a StudioFlow membership plan, you receive access to asynchronous design and frontend development services submitted through your designated project board. Requests are fulfilled sequentially (one active task at a time on Growth, or two active tasks on Scale).

1.2 Billing, Pauses & Cancellations

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance on a 31-day billing cycle. You may pause or cancel your subscription at any time through your billing portal prior to your renewal date. When paused, remaining days in your current 31-day cycle are banked and resume upon reactivation.

1.3 Refund Policy

Due to the custom nature of creative and engineering work, fees paid are non-refundable once design or development work has commenced on your account.

1.4 Intellectual Property Ownership

Upon full payment of your active subscription period, all custom designs, source code, and digital assets created specifically for you become your sole and exclusive intellectual property.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver our services and process payments, including your name, business email address, billing details (processed securely via Stripe), and project briefs submitted on your client board.

2.2 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to manage your subscription, communicate project updates, and deliver completed deliverables. We never sell, rent, or trade client data to third parties.

2.3 Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding these terms or wish to request deletion of your account records, contact us at hello@studioflow.agency.
